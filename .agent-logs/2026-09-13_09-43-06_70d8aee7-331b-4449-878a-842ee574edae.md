## What changed structurally

The old app was one page with a sidebar that swapped its right pane. Fathom is a **library → call** flow, so it's now two routes:

- **[/](frontend/src/app/page.tsx) — the homepage.** App bar (logo, "Search with AI…", Refer / Settings / Help & Feedback / star pill / avatar), the tab row (My Calls · Team Calls · Folders · Playlists), a thumbnail grid grouped under relative date headings, and the **Ask Recap** column on the right with suggestion chips and an "Ask anything…" input.
- **[/calls/[id]](frontend/src/app/calls/[id]/page.tsx) — the call.** Player on the left with `SUMMARY / TRANSCRIPT / ASK RECAP` tabs beneath it; right rail with the title, date, Add to Folder, the big Share bar, Attendees, Related, and Action Items with checkboxes and timestamps.

No sign-in anywhere — the avatar identifies the workspace rather than pretending to be a user.

The **flexible video window** is intact — drag the grip to resize, double-click to collapse, arrow keys to nudge, size remembered in `localStorage`. Search now navigates properly too: a hit pushes `/calls/<id>?t=<ms>` **and** fires a seek event, so jumping within the call you're already reading works rather than silently doing nothing.
