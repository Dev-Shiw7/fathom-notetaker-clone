The splash screen works. When someone opens the app, they see the animated Recall logo for about two seconds, and then it fades into the landing page. I checked this in a real browser.

**What you'll see**
- **Logo:** a gold ring draws itself, a glowing dot pops in and beats, and soft waves ripple outward.
- **Text:** "RECALL" appears letter by letter, with the tagline "Stay in the room. Keep the rest." fading in below it.
- **Timing:** the overlay was gone by about 2.25 seconds after load. The landing page's headline then types in, and 7 words had appeared by 4.2 seconds. The headline waits for the splash so its entrance isn't hidden behind the overlay.
- **No flash:** the splash is in the page's initial HTML, so the landing page never shows first.
- **Replays on reload:** it plays on every full page load, including a reload. It doesn't replay when you navigate back to the landing page from inside the app.
- **Reduced motion and no JavaScript:** with reduced motion on, the logo is static. With JavaScript off, the splash is hidden so the page isn't blocked.

The code is in [Splash.tsx](frontend/src/app/(marketing)/Splash.tsx). It's mounted from `page.tsx`, with its styles at the end of `landing.css` and a small timing change in `behavior.ts`. Nothing is committed yet.

I tested only on desktop Chrome, and mobile and a slow connection are untested. Say if you want it to play only once per browser session.
